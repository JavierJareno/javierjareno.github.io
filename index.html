About

Research Profile

I am a predoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Engineering at the School of Engineering, University of Cadiz. I currently hold an EMERGIA competitive predoctoral fellowship funded by the regional Government of Andalusia.

My research interests include green machine learning and deep learning, evolutionary algorithms, automatic software transformation, and AI-driven strategies for reducing emissions in hybrid and electric transport systems.
